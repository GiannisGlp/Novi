# Mac Brain Prototype Acceptance Criteria

## Gate M0 — Mac I/O

Camera, microphone and speakers operate through normalized interfaces and are observable.

## Gate M1 — Multimodal perception

Novi can combine visual and audio observations into structured evidence.

## Gate M2 — World state

Observations produce coherent, timestamped world state with uncertainty and provenance.

## Gate M3 — Memory

Relevant information can be stored and retrieved without confusing memory with current observation.

## Gate M4 — Cognition

Novi can interpret multimodal context and produce structured reasoning evidence.

## Gate M5 — Goals and planning

Novi can accept bounded goals, generate plans, track progress and replan when conditions change.

## Gate M6 — Bounded autonomy

Novi can continuously observe, decide, act through allowed virtual actions and stop safely.

## Gate M7 — Closed loop

Representative scenarios demonstrate observe → reason → act → observe behavior over repeated cycles.

## Gate M8 — Failure/recovery

Required device, model, stale-data and planning failures are handled explicitly and safely.

## Gate M9 — Evidence

All gates have reproducible evidence tied to code, configuration, scenarios and model/provider identity.

## Final v0.1 gate

All applicable gates pass and the prototype can operate continuously on the Mac without requiring the final robot hardware.
