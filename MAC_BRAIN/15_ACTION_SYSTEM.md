# Mac Brain Action System

## Objective

Convert validated plans into bounded, typed action proposals and route permitted actions to Mac outputs or the virtual body.

## Initial actions

- speak;
- play/stop audio;
- visual notification;
- virtual move/turn/stop;
- wait;
- observe;
- report status.

## Safety

Actions are typed, validated, permissioned and observable. The virtual body must reject unknown or malformed actions.
