# Mac Brain North Star

## Goal

Novi should behave as a continuously operating multimodal cognitive system on the Mac, using real sensory input and bounded action interfaces.

## Required capabilities

- see through the camera;
- hear through the microphone;
- speak through the speakers;
- identify and track relevant entities/events;
- maintain structured world state;
- maintain short-term and durable memory where appropriate;
- reason over multimodal evidence;
- represent uncertainty;
- accept and manage goals;
- generate bounded plans;
- propose and execute virtual actions;
- observe the consequences of actions;
- recover from tested failures;
- maintain provenance and observability.

## Explicit non-goals for v0.1

- physical motor control;
- final robot sensor suite;
- final production hardware;
- final model selection;
- unrestricted autonomous behavior;
- replacing deterministic safety with neural output.

## Definition of success

The system must demonstrate these capabilities in repeatable scenarios, not merely in a conversational demo.
