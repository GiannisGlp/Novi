# Mac Brain Cognition

## Objective

Turn validated observations, memory and goals into coherent internal interpretations and bounded proposals.

## Responsibilities

- contextual interpretation;
- multimodal synthesis;
- question/answer behavior;
- event interpretation;
- uncertainty representation;
- reasoning requests to specialist models;
- generation of candidate plans.

## Non-responsibilities

Cognition does not directly authorize physical actuation. It produces structured proposals consumed by deterministic downstream policy.
